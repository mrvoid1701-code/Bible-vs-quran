# Chronology 8 — Muhammad and the Seal of Prophethood

The storyline now reaches the claim that **closes** it on the Islamic
side: Muhammad as the final prophet, allegedly **foretold in the Bible**.

## The Qur'an's text

- Muhammad is "the **seal of the prophets**" (Q 33:40).
- He is said to be found "**written with them in the Torah and the
  Gospel**" (Q 7:157), and Jesus foretells a messenger "**whose name is
  Ahmad**" (Q 61:6).

## The alleged biblical foreshadowings (Muslim reading)

1. **Deut 18:15–18** — "a prophet **like Moses** from **among their
   brethren**" → read as the Ishmaelite/Arab line.
2. **John 14:16,26; 15:26; 16:7** — the **Paraclete** → read as Ahmad
   (the *paraklētos* → *periklytos* "praised one" theory).
3. **Deut 33:2 (Paran); Isa 29:12 ("he is not learned"); Song 5:16
   (*maḥamaddim*); Hag 2:7** — assorted prooftexts.

## The Christian / academic response

- **Deut 18 "brethren":** in Deuteronomy's own usage "brethren" =
  fellow **Israelites**; and the NT applies "the prophet like Moses" to
  **Jesus** (Acts 3:22). The reading is **grammatically possible but
  contextually weaker**.
- **The Paraclete:** the Greek is *paraklētos* ("Advocate/Comforter"),
  and the text **self-identifies it as the Holy Spirit** who would come
  **soon** and **dwell in the disciples** (John 14:17,26) — not a future
  Arabian prophet. The *periklytos* ("Ahmad") emendation has **no
  manuscript support whatsoever** — and by the dossier's own manuscript
  standard ([cap. 02](02-bible-textual-transmission.md),
  [cap. 06](06-debates-and-scholars.md)), **a reading with zero
  manuscript attestation cannot be asserted as the text**.
- The remaining prooftexts rest on **isolated words read out of context**.

## Arbiter verdict — Muhammad foretold?

**Present.** Islam: Muhammad is the foretold, final prophet. Christianity/
academy: the cited texts refer to Israelite prophecy, Jesus, or the Holy
Spirit, and the *periklytos* theory is text-critically baseless.

**Weigh.**
- *As historical/textual claims:* **weak.** The Paraclete-as-Ahmad reading
  has **no manuscript basis** (the very standard the dossier applied to
  the *Comma Johanneum*, applied evenly here); the Deut 18 reading is
  **contextually disfavoured**.
- *As faith claims:* **non-falsifiable.** "These passages secretly point
  to Muhammad" cannot be disproved, but neither can it be evidenced — and
  unfalsifiable is **not** the same as supported (Methodology §1.4, §4).
- *Finality / "seal":* a **theological axiom**, not a historical datum;
  it has **no pre-Islamic documentary footprint**.

**Judge.** On the **textual/historical question** — *do the biblical texts
foretell Muhammad?* — the evidence is **weak to negative**, decisively so
for the Paraclete claim by the dossier's **own evenly-applied manuscript
rule**. On the **theological claim** of finality, the arbiter does **not**
rule true or false (out of remit) but **does** record that it rests on
**doctrine, not on documentary evidence**. Symmetry holds: the same
scalpel that cut the Christian *Comma Johanneum* cuts the Muslim
*periklytos* reading.
