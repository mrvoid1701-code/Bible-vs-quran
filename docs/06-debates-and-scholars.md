# Debates and Scholars: Who Argues What

Reminder (Methodology §1.4): a debater "winning" a stage event is
**rhetoric, not evidence**. This chapter maps *which arguments belong to
whom* so the reader can trace claims to their sources, not crown winners.

---

## 1. Neutral / academic scholarship (the arbiter's baseline)

| Scholar | Field | Core relevant contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Bart D. Ehrman** | NT textual criticism | Variants & "orthodox corruptions"; *concedes* no core doctrine rests on a disputed text. Also skeptical of traditional Qur'an origins. |
| **Bruce Metzger** | NT textual criticism | Standard methodology; the early text is reconstructable. |
| **Larry Hurtado / Richard Bauckham** | Christian origins | Very early high Christology *within* Jewish monotheism. |
| **Daniel B. Wallace** | NT manuscripts | Manuscript abundance → high recoverability (Christian scholar, academically rigorous). |
| **François Déroche / Behnam Sadeghi / Asma Hilali** | Qur'anic manuscripts | Sanaa palimpsest; early ʿUthmanic standardization with pre-standard variation. |
| **Nicolai Sinai / Angelika Neuwirth / Gabriel S. Reynolds / Sean Anthony** | Qur'anic studies | Qur'an as a datable late-antique text; literary & historical analysis. |
| **John Wansbrough / Patricia Crone / Michael Cook** | Revisionist origins | Late-canonization thesis — *largely undercut* by early manuscript dating; now minority. |
| **Keith Small** | Comparative MS criticism | "Preservation by elimination" (Qur'an) vs. "by comparison" (NT). |

These are the voices an arbiter weights most, because they argue from
**physical and textual data** and publish under peer review.

## 2. Christian apologetic debaters

| Figure | Signature arguments |
|---|---|
| **William Lane Craig** | Philosophical defense of God + Trinity coherence; historical case for the resurrection. |
| **James White** | Manuscript abundance; defense against Islamic *tahrif*; *qiraʾat* & Sanaa used in reverse. |
| **Mike Licona / Gary Habermas** | "Minimal facts" historical argument for the resurrection. |
| **Jay Smith** | Aggressive use of Sanaa/Birmingham/*qiraʾat* against Qur'anic perfect-preservation claims. |
| **Nabeel Qureshi** (d. 2017) | Narrative/experiential + historical-Jesus case. |

Recurring Christian move: *concede* NT variants, then argue
**recoverability** + **early high Christology**, then **turn the
corruption standard back onto the Qur'an** (symmetry).

## 3. Muslim apologetic debaters

| Figure | Signature arguments |
|---|---|
| **Ahmed Deedat** (d. 2005) | Pioneered the popular *Bible-contradictions / tahrif* style. |
| **Zakir Naik** | Mass-audience *tahrif*; comparative-religion debating. |
| **Shabir Ally** | The most academically engaged; uses Ehrman & critical NT scholarship carefully. |
| **Yasir Qadhi** | Trained scholar; *qiraʾat*/*ahruf* defense; later *publicly nuanced* the popular preservation narrative — notable for intellectual honesty. |
| **Mohammed Hijab / Ali Dawah** | Contemporary online debate; philosophical *tawhid* vs. Trinity. |

Recurring Muslim move: deploy **Ehrman + the disputed passages** for
*tahrif*, and press the **logical simplicity of *tawhid*** against the
Trinity.

## 4. Honest concessions on each side (signal vs. noise)

A useful filter: weight arguments that the *other* side's own experts
concede.

- **Conceded toward the Muslim case:** the NT is not letter-perfect;
  Comma Johanneum, Mark 16:9–20, John 7:53–8:11 are not original; the
  Trinity is post-biblical in *formulation*. *(Conceded by mainstream
  Christian scholarship.)*
- **Conceded toward the Christian case:** the Qur'an's text was unified by
  **destroying** variant codices; the **Sanaa lower text** and the
  **canonical *qiraʾat*** are real internal variation; the strong
  revisionist "very late Qur'an" thesis is **weak**. *(Conceded or
  demonstrated by mainstream Qur'anic scholarship; some, like Qadhi, say
  so publicly.)*

The genuinely strong, defensible claims are exactly the ones **the
opposing experts do not dispute**. Everything that *only* one camp's
debaters assert should be treated as contested until traced to the data in
chapters [02](02-bible-textual-transmission.md)–[05](05-monotheism-vs-trinity.md).
