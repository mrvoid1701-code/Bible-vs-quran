# Chronology 1 — Creation

**Part III** walks the shared storyline from creation to the end. At each
stage: the Bible's text, the Qur'an's text, where they converge, where
they collide, then a present/weigh/judge verdict. The recurring finding
will be that **one continuous theological seam** — God's nature → sin →
forgiveness → Christ → the end — runs through every episode.

---

## The Bible's text

- **Genesis 1:** creation in **six days**, ordered — light (1); sky/
  firmament (2); land, sea, plants (3); sun, moon, stars (4); fish, birds
  (5); land animals and humankind, male and female (6); God **rests** (7),
  hallowing the Sabbath (Gen 2:2–3).
- **Genesis 2:** a second, differently-ordered account — the man formed
  **first** from dust, then the garden, then animals, then woman from his
  side.
- Creation is by divine speech ("And God said… and it was so"); the NT
  reads this as creation **from nothing** (Heb 11:3; John 1:3; Col 1:16).

## The Qur'an's text

- Creation in **six "days"** (*ayyam*, periods): Q 7:54; 10:3; 25:59.
- By sheer command: "**Be — and it is**" (*kun fayakun*, Q 2:117; 36:82).
- Heavens and earth were **a joined mass, then parted** (Q 21:30); the
  heaven "expanded" (Q 51:47).
- Humankind from **clay / dust / a drop** (Q 15:26; 22:5; 32:7–9); Adam
  taught "**the names**" (Q 2:31); the angels prostrate to Adam, Iblis
  refuses (Q 2:34).
- A breakdown in Q 41:9–12 lists earth in 2 + sustenance in 4 + heavens in
  2 — read additively this looks like **eight**, the classic *6-vs-8*
  exegetical crux (resolved by traditional exegetes as overlapping, not
  additive, periods).

## Convergence

Six creative periods; a single God creating by **fiat**, with **no rival
deity** and **no pre-existent co-creator**; humankind formed from **earth/
clay**; a primordial couple; a tempter figure.

## Collision

1. **Internal Genesis tension:** Gen 1 (plants day 3, *before* the sun day
   4; humans last) vs. Gen 2 (man *before* plants and animals) — a
   well-known source-critical seam in the Bible itself.
2. **The Qur'an's 6-vs-8 arithmetic** of Q 41:9–12 — an internal
   exegetical knot, paralleling Genesis's internal knot.
3. **Does God rest?** Gen 2:2–3 has God **resting** on the seventh day.
   Q 50:38 states creation occurred "**and no fatigue touched Us**" —
   which reads naturally as a **deliberate correction of Genesis**. This
   is a genuine theological divergence, not a copying detail: it flows
   straight from the [nature-of-God](09-nature-of-god.md) contrast
   (absolute transcendence vs. an anthropomorphic-friendly text).

## Arbiter verdict — creation

**Present.** Both: one God, six periods, creation by command, man from
earth. Bible adds Sabbath rest and a two-account structure; Qur'an adds
"join-then-part" and the *kun fayakun* formula.

**Weigh.**
- *Symmetric weakness:* each text carries an **internal ordering/number
  puzzle** (Genesis's two accounts; the Qur'an's 6-vs-8). Neither can use
  it against the other without conceding its own (Methodology §3).
- *Real divergence:* the **"God rests" vs. "no fatigue"** point is
  substantive and textual, and Q 50:38 looks corrective by design.
- *Out of remit:* concordance with modern cosmology/biology is argued
  apologetically on **both** sides; the arbiter does not rule on science-
  vs-text harmonization (Methodology §5).

**Judge.** On the **shared frame**: a draw — strikingly similar. On the
**internal puzzles**: a draw — both have one. On **"does God rest"**: a
**clean divergence**, with the Qur'an's wording functioning as an explicit
revision of Genesis; which portrayal is "better" depends on the prior
theology-of-God premise already shown to be non-arbitrable
([cap. 09](09-nature-of-god.md)). The seam appears immediately.
