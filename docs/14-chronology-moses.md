# Chronology 5 — Moses and the Exodus

Moses (Musa) is the **most-mentioned figure in the Qur'an**; the parallel
with the Bible is the closest in the whole storyline — which makes the
divergences especially diagnostic.

## Shared narrative

Birth under threat and the basket on the river; flight; the burning bush
and commission; the staff/serpent and the signs; confrontation with
Pharaoh; plagues; the deliverance through the sea and Pharaoh's
destruction; revelation at the mountain (Torah / the tablets); the people's
rebellion.

## The key divergences (text)

### 1. The golden calf — who is guilty?

- **Bible:** **Aaron himself makes the calf** (Exod 32:2–4).
- **Qur'an:** the calf is made by **al-Samiri**; **Aaron (Harun) is
  explicitly exonerated** — he warns the people and is innocent
  (Q 20:85–94).
- This protects the Islamic doctrine of **prophetic impeccability
  (*'isma*)** — a systematic, not incidental, rewrite of the moral roles.

### 2. Pharaoh's end

- **Qur'an-only details:** Pharaoh's **deathbed profession of faith is
  rejected** (Q 10:90–91) and "**We will preserve your body as a sign**"
  (Q 10:92). This has no biblical parallel; modern apologetic use of
  Egyptian mummies is **rhetoric, not proof** (Methodology §1.4).

### 3. The "Haman" question

- The Qur'an places a **Haman** in **Pharaoh's Egypt** as his minister,
  building a tower (Q 28:6, 38; 40:36–37).
- The only biblical Haman is a Persian official in **Esther**, ~a
  millennium later and a different empire — a standard **critical
  objection of anachronism**.
- **Muslim responses:** "Haman" may be a **title/office** or a common
  name, not the Esther figure; absence from extant Egyptian records is not
  disproof. Both positions are presented; neither is decisive.

## Arbiter verdict — Moses

**Present.** The richest shared narrative in the storyline; divergences
concentrated on **moral roles** and **a few Qur'an-only specifics**.

**Weigh.**
- *Systematic, theology-driven:* Aaron's exoneration is **internally
  consistent** with *'isma* — a doctrine, not a copying slip.
- *Genuinely contested:* the **Haman** anachronism is a **real academic
  objection** with a **non-trivial Muslim rebuttal**; the *prima facie*
  reading favors the anachronism charge, but it is **not a knockout** and
  the dossier records it as **unresolved/contested** (Methodology §4 — an
  argument that cannot be cleanly closed is not awarded as a win).
- *Out of remit:* mummy/concordist arguments — rhetoric, set aside.

**Judge.** On the **shared core**: a strong draw — the closest agreement
so far. On **Aaron and the calf**: a clean, **doctrine-driven divergence**
(impeccability), internally coherent on each side. On **Haman**: the
balance leans toward the **critical objection** but is **formally
contested** and therefore **not scored as decisive**. The seam reappears
precisely where Islamic prophetology requires sinless prophets — setting
up the next chapter.
