# Chronology 9 — Eschatology: The End

The storyline closes where it began — with the seam intact.

## Striking agreement

Both texts affirm: a coming end of the age; **bodily resurrection of the
dead**; a universal **Day of Judgment**; **heaven and hell** as real
destinations; the **return of Jesus**; a great **deceiver** figure; and
**final, perfect justice**.

What each actually says:

- **Bible:** the trumpet and the dead raised (1 Thess 4:16; 1 Cor
  15:52); the **resurrection body** (1 Cor 15:42–44); judgment "**each
  according to what he has done**" (Matt 25:31–46; Rev 20:12) — note:
  even the Bible judges *by deeds as evidence*; new heaven and new earth,
  "**no more death**", and "**they will see His face**" (Rev 21:1–4;
  22:4).
- **Qurʾan:** the trumpet (*ṣūr*, Q 39:68); the Quaking and "an **atom's
  weight**" weighed (Q 99:7–8); the **Balance** (*mīzān*, Q 23:102–103;
  101:6–9); the records given in right/left hand (Q 69:19–25); and —
  correcting a common caricature — a **beatific vision**: "**faces, that
  Day, radiant, looking at their Lord**" (Q 75:22–23). *(The Bridge
  (*ṣirāṭ*) and many hell/paradise specifics are **ḥadīth**, flagged as
  tradition, not Qurʾan.)*

> Precision (Methodology §1): the **Dajjal** is principally **hadith**,
> not Qur'an; the **Antichrist** is principally the **Johannine epistles**
> and tradition, lightly in the NT. Both "deceiver" motifs are flagged as
> **more traditional than scriptural**, on each side equally.

## The decisive divergences

### 1. The criterion of salvation — the seam's terminus
- **Islam:** judgment by **faith + deeds**, under God's sovereign
  **mercy/will**; **no atonement** is needed (direct downstream of
  [cap. 11](11-chronology-fall.md) and [cap. 09 §2.4](09-nature-of-god.md)).
- **Christianity:** salvation by **grace through Christ's atoning work**,
  received by faith — because the [cap. 11](11-chronology-fall.md) Fall
  created a debt only the [cap. 16](16-chronology-jesus.md) cross pays.

The **same fork from the garden** determines the **gate of paradise**.
This is the seam's endpoint: one continuous logical thread from creation
to judgment.

### 2. Jesus' role at the end
- **Islam:** returns as a **Muslim**, breaks the cross, defeats the
  Dajjal, affirms Islam, then dies.
- **Christianity:** returns as the **divine Judge and King**.
- Same figure, **mutually exclusive roles** — the
  [cap. 05](05-monotheism-vs-trinity.md)/[16](16-chronology-jesus.md)
  divergence projected onto the last day.

### 3. Mediation
- Islam — limited **intercession** (*shafāʿa*) by God's leave;
  Christianity — **Christ the one mediator** (1 Tim 2:5). This is the
  [cap. 16](16-chronology-jesus.md) divergence, not a new one.

## Slogans that dissolve on inspection

Two popular contrasts are **overdrawn** and, by the dossier's method
(dissolve the slogan, keep the real seam), must be corrected — **against
both sides**:

- **"Islam = works, Christianity = grace."** False as stated. The Bible
  judges **by deeds** (Matt 25; James 2:24 "justified by works") *and*
  teaches grace (Eph 2:8–9); Islam teaches **deeds** *and* that "**none
  enters Paradise by his deeds — only by God's mercy**" (a sound ḥadīth)
  and rests on God's *raḥma* ([cap. 09](09-nature-of-god.md)). **Both
  hold faith, works, and grace together.** The irreducible difference is
  **not** works-vs-grace; it is **atonement vs. no-atonement**
  ([cap. 11](11-chronology-fall.md)/[16](16-chronology-jesus.md)).
- **"Qurʾanic paradise = physical, biblical heaven = spiritual."** False.
  The Qurʾan has the **beatific vision** (Q 75:22–23) and the Bible has a
  **physical new earth, feasting, and no more death** (Rev 21). Both are
  **physical *and* God-centred**; the *hūr* verses (Q 44:54; 55:56) are
  also linguistically debated and are **not** the caricature.

## Arbiter verdict — the end

**Present.** A **large shared eschatological frame**; decisive divergence
on the **mechanism of salvation** and **Christ's identity/role**.

**Weigh.**
- *Strong shared structure:* resurrection, judgment, heaven/hell,
  returning Christ, deceiver, final justice, **and even a beatific
  vision** — substantive, not vague.
- *Slogans dissolved:* "works vs. grace" and "physical vs. spiritual
  paradise" are **both false as stated** — corrected against both sides.
- *The one irreducible divergence:* **atonement vs. no-atonement** (plus
  Christ's identity at the end) — the **logical terminus of the
  [cap. 11](11-chronology-fall.md) fork**, which cannot be true both ways.
- *Symmetric flags:* the deceiver motifs (Dajjāl / Antichrist) and many
  paradise/hell specifics are **traditional more than scriptural** on
  **both** sides.

**Judge.** On the **eschatological scaffolding**: a strong **draw** —
notably similar, even more so once the false contrasts are removed. On the
**one real divergence** (atonement and Christ's identity): **not
arbitrable** — each system is **internally coherent**, and which is
**true** is a theological-truth question the arbiter has, from the start,
declined ([Methodology §5](01-methodology.md)). The honest yield of the
whole chronology is that **most "knockout" contrasts shrink on inspection
to one genuine, non-decidable seam.**

## The whole arc — one seam, restated

| Stage | The single dividing question |
|---|---|
| [God's nature](09-nature-of-god.md) | Simple/transcendent, or triune/incarnational love? |
| [Creation](10-chronology-creation.md) | Does God "rest" / enter creaturely terms? |
| [The Fall](11-chronology-fall.md) | Is sin inherited (→ redeemer needed)? |
| [Flood](12-chronology-flood.md) / [Abraham](13-chronology-abraham.md) | Salvation by lineage, or by personal faith? |
| [Moses](14-chronology-moses.md) / [David](15-chronology-david-prophets.md) | Are prophets fallible, or impeccable? |
| [Jesus](16-chronology-jesus.md) | Did the cross happen, and what is Jesus? |
| [Muhammad](17-chronology-muhammad.md) | Is revelation sealed, and is he foretold? |
| The End | Saved by atonement, or by deeds under mercy? |

**Final word of Part III.** The deepened reading sharpens, not softens,
the result. Most of the famous "knockout" contrasts — guilt-transfer,
sinful-vs-sinless prophets, works-vs-grace, physical-vs-spiritual heaven,
even "did Jesus die" — **shrink on close inspection**, because each
scripture's *own text* is more internally mixed than the slogans admit
(Ezek 18 inside the Bible; repenting prophets inside the Qurʾan; grace
inside Islam; deeds inside the Gospel). What remains after the slogans
dissolve is **one continuous, traceable seam**: **inherited need →
atonement → the identity and death of Christ → the gate of salvation**.
On the **bare historical questions** (the crucifixion *as event*;
manuscript-based prophecy claims) the neutral method **does lean**, by the
**same rule applied to both sides**. On that **one theological seam**, the
arbiter's consistent verdict is the honest one: **internally coherent on
each side, and not decidable by evidence or logic alone** — which is
exactly what neutrality, held to the end, looks like.
