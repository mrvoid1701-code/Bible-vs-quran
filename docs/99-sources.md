# Sources and Further Reading

These are the standard works behind the claims in this dossier. They are
listed so every assertion can be traced and independently checked. They
represent a range of positions on purpose — an arbiter reads all sides.

## Bible — text & transmission
- Bruce M. Metzger & Bart D. Ehrman, *The Text of the New Testament*.
- Bart D. Ehrman, *Misquoting Jesus*; *The Orthodox Corruption of
  Scripture*; *How Jesus Became God*.
- Daniel B. Wallace (ed.), *Revisiting the Corruption of the New
  Testament*.
- Emanuel Tov, *Textual Criticism of the Hebrew Bible*.
- The Dead Sea Scrolls scholarship (Great Isaiah Scroll, 1QIsaᵃ).

## Christian origins & Christology
- Larry W. Hurtado, *Lord Jesus Christ: Devotion to Jesus in Earliest
  Christianity*.
- Richard Bauckham, *Jesus and the God of Israel*.
- Bart D. Ehrman, *How Jesus Became God* (counter-position).
- J. N. D. Kelly, *Early Christian Doctrines* (Nicaea → Chalcedon).

## Trinity — philosophy & coherence
- Thomas McCall & Michael Rea (eds.), *Philosophical and Theological
  Essays on the Trinity*.
- Richard Swinburne, *The Christian God*.
- Brian Leftow / Peter van Inwagen / Dale Tuggy — contrasting analytic
  positions (defenses **and** critiques).

## Qur'an — text & transmission
- François Déroche, *Qur'ans of the Umayyads*; *The One and the Many*.
- Behnam Sadeghi & Mohsen Goudarzi, "Ṣanʿāʾ 1 and the Origins of the
  Qurʾān" (*Der Islam*, 2012).
- Asma Hilali, *The Sanaa Palimpsest*.
- Keith E. Small, *Textual Criticism and Qurʾān Manuscripts*.
- Nicolai Sinai, *The Qur'an: A Historical-Critical Introduction*.
- Angelika Neuwirth, *The Qur'an and Late Antiquity*.
- Gabriel Said Reynolds, *The Qur'an and the Bible*.
- John Wansbrough, *Quranic Studies*; Patricia Crone & Michael Cook,
  *Hagarism* (revisionist — included for completeness; now minority).

## Tahrif — Islamic theology of corruption
- Classical: Ibn Hazm (*tahrif al-nass*); al-Razi & others
  (*tahrif al-maʿna*); Ibn Khaldun.
- Modern survey: Gordon Nickel, *Narratives of Tampering in the Earliest
  Commentaries on the Qurʾān*.
- Abdullah Saeed, "The Charge of Distortion of Jewish and Christian
  Scriptures" (*The Muslim World*).

## On using debates responsibly
Public debates (Shabir Ally, James White, Jay Smith, Yasir Qadhi, Mohammed
Hijab, William Lane Craig, Mike Licona, et al.) are useful as **maps of
arguments**, not as evidence. Always trace a debate claim back to the
academic literature above before weighting it.

> here are accurate to the best of standard reference knowledge; exact
> imprints are not reproduced from memory to avoid false precision.
