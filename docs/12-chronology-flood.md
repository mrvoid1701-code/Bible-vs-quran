# Chronology 3 — The Flood and Noah

## The Bible's text

- Gen 6–9: human wickedness; an ark of stated dimensions; **40 days** of
  rain, waters prevailing **150 days**; **all flesh dies** except those in
  the ark — **eight souls**: Noah, his wife, his three sons (Shem, Ham,
  Japheth) and their wives. **Noah's entire household is saved.**
- A rainbow covenant follows (Gen 9).

## The Qur'an's text

- Nuh preaches for **"950 years"** to an unbelieving people (Q 29:14);
  builds the ark under inspiration (Q 11:37–38).
- The signal of onset: "**the oven gushed forth**" (*fara al-tannur*,
  Q 11:40).
- **A son of Noah refuses** to board — "I will take refuge on a
  mountain" — and **drowns** (Q 11:42–43).
- **Noah's wife** is named among those who disbelieved and were lost
  (Q 66:10).
- Scope (global vs. local) is left open and is **debated in tafsir**.

## Collision

The decisive divergence is **who was saved**:

| | Bible | Qur'an |
|---|---|---|
| Noah's wife | saved | **lost** (Q 66:10) |
| Noah's sons | all three saved | **one son drowns** (Q 11:43) |
| Salvation principle | household / lineage | **faith over kinship** |

The Qur'an's version is internally purposeful: it **dramatizes the
[cap. 11](11-chronology-fall.md) principle** — no one is saved by family
tie; each stands on personal faith ("he is not of your family; his conduct
is unrighteous", Q 11:46). It reads as a **theological correction** of
Genesis's household-salvation motif, fully consistent with Q 35:18.

## Arbiter verdict — the Flood

**Present.** Shared event (righteous Noah, ark, deluge, deliverance);
divergent on the survivors and on the *principle* the story teaches.

**Weigh.**
- *Symmetric, out of remit:* the **historicity of a global flood** is
  rejected by mainstream geology/archaeology; this pressure falls on
  **both** texts equally (Methodology §3, §5) and decides nothing between
  them.
- *Real divergence, internally consistent:* the lost son and wife are
  **deliberate** in the Qur'an, locking the flood narrative onto the same
  individual-responsibility seam as the Fall.

**Judge.** On **historicity**: a wash — neither is favored; both face the
same external problem. On **internal theological coherence**: the Qur'an's
account is **tightly integrated** with its own anthropology (faith over
lineage), while the Bible's is integrated with its **covenant-household**
theology. No evidentiary winner; a clear, traceable continuation of the
seam.
