# Final Arbiter Verdict

This synthesizes the four contested fields. The verdict separates what the
evidence **can** decide from what it **cannot**, and — where it can — names
the stronger position with its caveats.

---

## 1. Scoreboard by question (not by religion)

| Question | Stronger position | Confidence | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is the NT's early text **recoverable**? | **Christian (reliability)** | High | 5,800+ Greek MSS; early attestation; variants traceable *because* of abundance |
| Is the NT **letter-perfect / unaltered**? | **Critic** | High | A few weighty passages (Comma Johanneum, Mark 16:9–20, John 7:53–8:11) are demonstrably later; conceded by Christian scholars |
| Was the Qur'an's *rasm* **fixed very early**? | **Muslim (early stability)** | High | Birmingham + early hijazi MSS match ʿUthmanic *rasm* |
| Has the Qur'an reached us with **zero variation**? | **Critic** | High | Sanaa lower text + canonical *qiraʾat* are physical/internal counter-evidence |
| Is the Bible **wholesale fabricated** (strong *tahrif*)? | **Defense (charge fails)** | High | Manuscript evidence refutes it; not even the Qur'an's most natural claim |
| Does the **symmetry test** favor either side? | **Neither** | High | An even corruption standard wounds both "perfect preservation" claims about equally |
| Which text **directly states** its monotheology? | **Qur'an → strict *tawhid*** | High | Explicit; internally consistent on its own terms |
| Is the **Trinity a flat contradiction**? | **Christian (charge fails)** | Medium–High | Essence/person distinction blocks "1=3"; but rests on contested metaphysics |
| Is the Trinity **explicitly stated in the Bible**? | **Critic** | High | It is a cumulative post-biblical *synthesis*; best single proof-text is spurious |

## 2. The three demanded verdict styles, combined

**Present (both best cases).** Each chapter states the strongest honest
form of both sides — not the strawman.

**Weigh (strong / weak / contested).**
- *Empirically strong & cross-conceded:* NT recoverability; early Qur'anic
  *rasm*; NT not letter-perfect; Qur'an not variant-free; strong
  revisionism is weak; bare "1=3" objection fails.
- *Empirically weak:* "the Bible was wholesale fabricated"; "the Qur'an
  has literally zero variants"; "the Trinity is stated plainly in
  Scripture"; "the Trinity is a self-evident logical contradiction."
- *Genuinely contested:* classical Muslim consensus on *nass* vs. *maʿna*;
  the meaning of Q 5:116's triad; high vs. escalating early Christology;
  which Trinitarian model (Latin / Social / relative-identity) is best.

**Judge (name the stronger side where warranted).**
1. **Transmission:** symmetric. *Both* scriptures are early and
   substantively recoverable; *neither* is the letter-perfect, variant-
   free object its popular apologists claim. The corruption debate is
   almost always "won" by whoever is permitted an **asymmetric standard** —
   to which **no one is entitled**. **Net: a draw on the honest standard.**
2. **The *tahrif* charge specifically:** the **strong** form (Bible
   fabricated) **fails**; the **weak** form (Bible humanly transmitted,
   variant-bearing, partly composite) **succeeds — and is conceded by
   Christianity itself**, which redefines "preservation" as recoverability.
   Applied evenly, the weak form **also** lands on the Qur'an.
3. **Monotheism vs. Trinity — textual:** on *its own text*, the **Qur'an
   wins decisively** for stating its theology explicitly and consistently.
   The **Bible is two-stranded**; the Trinity is the better *synthesis* of
   *all* its data than strict unitarianism is, but it is **constructed,
   not stated**. So: *clarity* → Qur'an; *richness/tension* → Bible.
4. **Monotheism vs. Trinity — logic:** strict monotheism is **simpler and
   not contradictory**. The Trinity is **not a formal contradiction** but
   is **metaphysically heavy and internally contested**. Advantage on
   *parsimony* → strict monotheism; the *“irrational”* charge → fails.

---

## 3. What the arbiter explicitly will **not** rule

- Whether God exists, is one, or is triune **in reality**. (Not a
  historical/textual question — Methodology §5.)
- Whether divine revelation *ought to be* textually simple (favoring the
  Qur'an's profile) or developed/multi-stranded (the Bible's profile).
  That is a theological value judgment, not an evidentiary one.
- Which religion is "true." Out of scope, by design.

---

## 4. One-paragraph bottom line

On the **historical/textual** front the contest is **closer to a draw than
either camp admits**: both scriptures are early and recoverable in
substance, and both fall short of the flawless-preservation myth their
popular defenders sell — the Bible *by visible variants*, the Qur'an *by
suppressed ones*. The Muslim *tahrif* argument succeeds only in its **weak,
symmetric form** (which boomerangs) and **fails in its strong form**. On
the **theological** front the two scriptures are simply **different in
kind**: the Qur'an is **textually unambiguous** about strict monotheism,
while the Bible is **textually layered**, with the Trinity as a coherent-
but-constructed synthesis rather than a stated doctrine. The bare charge
that the Trinity is *irrational* **fails**; the charge that it is *not
plainly biblical* **succeeds**. Beyond that line, the questions stop being
historical — and so does the arbiter's authority.
